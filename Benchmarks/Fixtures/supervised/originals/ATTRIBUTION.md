# Wisconsin diagnostic breast cancer data

Source: [UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin Diagnostic dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/17/breast+cancer+wisconsin+diagnostic), DOI [10.24432/C5DW2B](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5DW2B).

UCI supplies this citation: Wolberg, W., Mangasarian, O., Street, N., & Street, W. 1993. Breast Cancer Wisconsin Diagnostic. UCI Machine Learning Repository. The original `wdbc.names` identifies William H. Wolberg, W. Nick Street, and Olvi L. Mangasarian as creators.

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the [full license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).

`wdbc.data` and `wdbc.names` are unmodified source bytes. The accompanying schema names the original columns and defines a numeric target mapping of benign to 0 and malignant to 1. Feature values and source diagnoses remain unchanged. These additions do not imply endorsement by the creators or UCI.
