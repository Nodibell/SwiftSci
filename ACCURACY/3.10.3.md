# SwiftSci 3.10.3 Accuracy & Numerical Parity

> [!WARNING]
> **Historical Validity & `v3.13.2` Audit Notice**: Legacy `--research` runners (`SwiftSciBenchmarks --research`, `Benchmarks/Python/accuracy_benchmarks.py`) were **retired and removed in `v3.13.2`**. In addition, `3.10.3` predated the `3.11.0` Audits 1–15 remediations (`TargetEncoder` smoothing, TreeSHAP centering, Kalman symmetry), `3.13.1` statistical/CSV fixes, and `3.13.2` ARIMA/SARIMA (G-033/G-037) fixes (`ARIMA(1,1,1)` uses Hannan-Rissanen conditional OLS, not Gaussian MLE).

Historical numerical and predictive research measurements comparing **SwiftSci 3.10.3** (Swift 6, Apple Silicon Accelerate BLAS/LAPACK & Metal) against **NumPy 2.3.5**, **Scikit-Learn 1.8.0**, **SciPy 1.17.1**, **Statsmodels 0.14.6**, **NLTK 3.10.3**, and **PyTorch 2.11.0**.

## Correctness & Mathematical Enhancements in 3.10.3

| Subsystem | Prior Behavior (`3.10.2`) | `3.10.3` Enhancement | Numerical Impact & Parity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **One-Way ANOVA (`SwiftStats`)** | Naive $\sum x^2$ formulas suffered catastrophic cancellation on large offsets ($x_i \approx 10^9$). | Blocked centered moments (`CenteredMoments.swift`) and exact Decimal ANOVA (`Stats+DecimalANOVA.swift`). | Exact $F$-statistic and $p$-value stability at $10^9$ offset parity with SciPy. |
| **Dot Products (`SwiftStats`)** | Standard floating-point accumulation risked error compounding in ill-conditioned vectors. | Added `DotProductAccuracy.compensated` (Kahan-Babuška-Neumaier) and `.exact` accumulation paths. | Precision maintained up to $10^{-16}$ in high-dimensional cosine distances and covariance. |
| **PCA Variance & Cosine Guard (`SwiftCluster`)** | PCA EVR normalized over selected components only ($\sum \text{EVR} = 1.0$); zero-magnitude cosine gave `NaN`. | Normalized EVR across all fitted eigenvalues/singular values ($\sum \lambda_i$); explicit `0.0` zero-magnitude guard. | Exact 6-decimal EVR parity with Scikit-Learn and zero `NaN` ranking contamination. |
| **Regression State (`SwiftML`)** | Warm-start state or initial weights could be overwritten on re-fit. | Retained model state, priors, and bias terms with compensated residual gradients. | Predictable iterative convergence and safe transfer learning. |
| **Parquet Conformance (`SwiftDataFrame`)** | Custom compact page layout differed from Apache Parquet specification. | Conformed page headers, dictionary encoding, and packed boolean bit unpacking to Apache Parquet spec. | 100% interoperability with PyArrow, DuckDB, and HuggingFace datasets. |
