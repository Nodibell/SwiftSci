## Correctness & Mathematical Enhancements in 3.13.3

| Subsystem | Prior Defect / Gap | `3.13.3` Resolution | Verification Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G-042 / G-043: `MLP` BLAS Mini-Batch, Extreme Logits & Wide-Layer Parity** | Scalar training inner loops, numerical overflow on extreme logits, partial-batch gradient scaling drift, and `<= 512` width cutoff on batched prediction. | Rebuilt training with Accelerate BLAS mini-batch forward/backward passes, log-sum-exp softmax/BCE stability, partial-batch normalization, and `256 KiB` scratch-budgeted batched prediction up to width `8,192`. | Central finite-difference gradient checks `< 1e-6` across ReLU/Sigmoid/Tanh (binary, 3-class, 5-class, MSE; full & partial batches), extreme logit stability (`±1000`), wide-layer parity (`768`, `2048`, `4096`), and 768-D `MLPClassifier` (`train_accuracy = 1.0000`). |
| **G-044: `MLP` Input Rectangularity & Single-Class Consistency** | Ragged feature rows, mismatched validation pairs, and single-class prediction inconsistencies lacked uniform validation. | Enforced rectangular feature/target checks across `fit`, `predict`, `predictProbability`, and `validationFeatures`/`validationTargets`, plus consistent single-class predictions. | Verified by `SwiftSci3133RegressionTests` dimension-mismatch and single-class assertions. |
| **G-045: `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` Multiclass & Rectangularity** | Histogram boosting only handled binary targets and lacked uniform ragged-row validation. | Added multiclass softmax gradient boosting (`K > 2`) preserving arbitrary non-zero-based integer class labels (e.g., `[10, 20, 30]`) and rectangularity checks across regressor and classifier paths. | Verified 3-class non-zero-based convergence, probability simplex invariants, and mismatch error gates. |
| **G-046: `LinearSVC` Zero/Negative Epochs & Rectangularity** | Non-positive `epochs` could fail or enter invalid loops; feature row widths were not uniformly validated. | Clamped `max(0, epochs)` and added rectangular feature checks across CPU and GPU `fit`, `decisionFunction`, and `predict`. | Verified on CPU and GPU `LinearSVC` paths (`epochs: 0`, `epochs: -5`, ragged rows). |
| **G-047: `PlattScaling` & `CalibratedClassifier` Stability** | Initial slope `a` / sigmoid saturation risk, missing unfitted checks, multiclass handling, and binary label assumptions. | Set positive initial slope `a = 1.0`, added overflow-safe `sigmoid`, enforced `modelNotFitted` and binary-only guards, and preserved arbitrary binary labels (e.g., `[-1, 1]`). | Verified extreme score calibration (`±1000`), unfitted/multiclass rejections, and `[-1, 1]` label preservation. |
| **G-048: `SwiftVision` YOLOv8 Un-Letterboxing, Annotation Clamping & `IoU` Safety** | Non-square `YOLOv8Detector.detect(image:)` letterbox coordinate drift, coupled corner clamping in `YOLOAnnotationParser.parse`, and negative areas in `BoundingBox.iou(with:)`. | Un-letterboxed `YOLOv8Detector.detect(image:)` boxes to normalized `[0, 1]` original-image coordinates, clamped `YOLOAnnotationParser.parse` edges independently, and clamped `BoundingBox.iou(with:)` area spans non-negative. | Verified on `640×360` / `360×640` detection, border-clamping, and inverted-box `IoU` tests. |
| **G-049: `SwiftVision` Detector Thresholds & Folder Profiling** | `NeuralObjectDetector(yoloDetector:)` overwrote custom detector thresholds, and `VisionImageClassifier.profileFolder` collided on duplicate filenames and averaged across invalid images. | Preserved `YOLOv8Detector` thresholds in `NeuralObjectDetector(yoloDetector:)`, disambiguated `profileFolder` sample keys by relative path across symlinks/splits, and averaged dimensions over valid decoded images. | Verified by threshold-preservation, split-layout collision, and valid-image averaging tests. |
| **Documentation Polish: Core ML Export Tables (`SwiftML`)** | DocC tables still marked `GradientBoostedTreesRegressor`, `MLPClassifier`/`MLPRegressor`, and `.mlpackage` bundles as deferred. | Updated `CoreMLExportable` and `CoreMLExporter` DocC documentation tables to reflect current supported exports. | Verified via `100.00%` (`2,551/2,551`) DocC coverage scan and unified DocC build. |
| **G-050 / G-051: Gemma 4 BOS Deduplication & `generation_config.json` EOS Union** | Duplicate `<bos>` / `<s>` emission in `ChatTemplate.gemma4` / `TransformerDecoder.generationPrompt`, and missed `eos_token_id` entries from `generation_config.json`. | Deduplicated leading BOS tokens when `SentencePieceBPETokenizer` already encodes them, fetched optional `generation_config.json` in `ModelDownloader`, and unioned scalar/array `eos_token_id` values safely. | Verified by `SwiftNLPTests` and `SwiftLLMTests` Gemma 4 / tokenizer / loader tests. |
| **G-038 / G-052: `ReActAgent` Thinking Tags & Action-Before-Final-Answer** | Gemma 4 `<\|channel>thought...<channel\|>` and `<think>` blocks, `Observation:` spillover into `Action Input`, and hallucinated `Final Answer` after `Action` in the same turn. | Extracted `<\|channel>thought` and `<think>` blocks cleanly, halted `Action Input` capture at `Observation:`, and prioritized `Action` execution when it precedes `Final Answer`. | Verified by `ReActAgentTests` inline, multi-block, empty, unclosed, and multi-turn execution tests. |
| **G-053 / Release Polish (`SwiftSciCLI` & Report Unification)** | `SwiftSciCLI` version banner and two-tier `ACCURACY` / `PERFORMANCE` reports needed `3.13.3` alignment. | Updated `SwiftSciCLI` to `3.13.3` and published unified two-tier `3.13.3` accuracy, performance, and deep audit reports. | Verified across CLI version, DocC portal, and Cookbook `71/71` verification. |
