## Highlights & What Changed in 3.13.2

- **Production-Default Standardized Campaign (`4,810` Validated Samples)**: Added `standard-production-default` (`5` process batches, `700` samples) alongside `legacy-capped` (`3` process batches) for `standard`, `nist`, `public-data`, `migration`, and `tabular-migration`.
- **Correctness-Cleared Scientific Benchmarking**: Cross-engine scientific speedups are reported **only** for stages cleared by scientific accuracy and comparator-equivalence checks (excluding the unmatched 500-point optimized Holt-Winters diagnostic where SwiftSci optimizes SSE and statsmodels optimizes likelihood).
- **G-033 / G-037 Forecasting Hardening**: Added non-finite and MA/SMA polynomial validation, AutoARIMA Schur–Cohn sign alignment, and observed-level one-step `fittedValues` reconstruction for ARIMA/SARIMA/ARIMAX without regressing matched Hannan-Rissanen speed (`0.039 ms` fit at `500` pts; `2.42 ms` fit at `50k` pts).
- **Retirement of Legacy `--research` Claims**: Replaced unvalidated in-process `--research` metrics with process-isolated `bench.py` and `scientific_validation_suite.py` campaigns.

## Methodology & Reproducibility

- **Timing Boundaries**: Worker wall time covers materialized output (CSV parsing is inside timed reads). Fixture setup, startup, imports, 2 warmups, and validation are excluded. Standardized batches use 5 measured samples per process (3 or 5 processes).
- **Interpretation Caveats**: Ratios are suppressed where timing falls below 1 µs (`nist-mean/variance/stddev`) or where objectives differ (e.g., 500-point optimized Holt-Winters SSE `0.263 ms` vs statsmodels `47.16 ms`). Whole-process Peak RSS in raw reports includes runtime/library startup overhead.
- **Reproduce & Audit**: Start from [tagged source `v3.13.2`](https://github.com/Nodibell/SwiftSci/tree/v3.13.2), follow the [Runner Guide](../Benchmarks/README.md), inspect raw fingerprints and full tables in [`performance.deep.md`](../Benchmarks/Reports/v3.13.2/performance.deep.md), or review validation gates in the [Companion Accuracy Report](../ACCURACY/3.13.2.md).
