# SwiftSci 3.10.3 Performance Overview

> [!WARNING]
> **Historical Validity & `v3.13.2` Audit Notice**: Recorded under Methodology v2 (`3` rounds × `7` iterations, `2` warmups, `seed=42`) on Apple Silicon `arm64` (`macOS 15`) with Swift Release (`-O -whole-module-optimization`) against Python `3.11.9` (`NumPy 2.3.5`, `Pandas 3.0.2`, `Scikit-Learn 1.4.2`, `Statsmodels 0.14.2`, `SHAP 0.44.1`, `PyTorch 2.11.0`). Legacy `--research` runners were **retired in `v3.13.2`**: `Holt-Winters >5000×` (SSE vs likelihood), `ARIMA(1,1,1) >9000×` (Hannan-Rissanen OLS vs Statsmodels MLE), `SwiftLLM 0.125 ms vs 1.450 ms`, and `Kiraa` 1M-row runs are non-comparable or experimental, and whole-process `Peak RSS` includes Python interpreter overhead (~148–412 MiB).

---

## Highlights & What Changed in 3.10.3

- **Compact Numeric Storage (`DenseColumn`)**: Contiguous numerical buffers with separate null validity masks replaced per-element optional boxing across tabular pipelines:
  - **Target Vector Extraction**: **`0.055 ms`** (vs `3.96 ms` in `3.10.2`, **72.0× faster**).
  - **Min–Max Scaling**: **`0.751 ms`** (vs `33.17 ms` in `3.10.2`, **44.1× faster**).
  - **Standard Scaling**: **`2.79 ms`** (vs `28.39 ms` in `3.10.2`, **10.2× faster**).
  - **Row Filtering / Group Sum / CSV Read (`100k`)**: **`0.217 ms`** (**7.48×** vs `3.10.2`), **`0.372 ms`** (**4.28×**), and **`6.80 ms`** (**1.91×**).
  - **Whole-Process Peak RSS**: Dropped from `186.4 MiB` (`3.10.2`) to **`34.4 MiB`** (**81% reduction**, `4.3×` lower whole-process RSS than Pandas `148.0 MiB`).
- **Numerical & Interop Hardening**: Added blocked centered moments ANOVA (`CenteredMoments.swift`, `Stats+DecimalANOVA.swift`), compensated Kahan-Babuška dot products, PCA total-variance normalization, zero-magnitude cosine guards, warm-start regression state preservation, and Apache Parquet specification conformance.
