> **Release Status — Historical (Superseded by v3.13.3)** (`v3.13.2`, [`8039d02`](https://github.com/Nodibell/SwiftSci/commit/8039d02f9d63c27022a794dfff5bd43d2e8a9710)): Audited across `6/6` standardized conformance certificates (`4,810` measurements), `39/39` scientific validation workloads, `3/3` rolling-origin backtests, `2/2` expected-error checks, `202/202` SwiftSci CPU acceptance cases, `60` pretrained inspection + `40` workflow mutation controls, `240/240` Python tests (including MLX GPU), and `14/14` DocC archives (`2,551/2,551` public declarations).

## Correctness & Mathematical Enhancements in 3.13.2

| Subsystem | Prior Defect / Gap | `3.13.2` Resolution | Verification Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G-033: ARIMA / SARIMA / AutoARIMA Validation** | Missing non-finite intermediate guards, invalid MA/SMA polynomial validation, and Schur–Cohn sign ambiguity. | Added finite and invertibility checks (failed fits clear fitted state) and aligned AutoARIMA Schur–Cohn coefficient sign convention. | Verified by `2/2` expected-error checks and AutoARIMA sign/stability gates. |
| **G-037: One-Step Fitted Values & Residuals** | Differenced ARIMA/SARIMA models suffered open-loop integration drift in `fittedValues`. | Reconstructs one-step `fittedValues` from observed levels after warmup (`d` for ARIMA, `d + D*s` for SARIMA); ARIMAX includes exogenous terms. | `4` Swift regression tests, `9` scientific workloads, `6` rolling-origin folds (`0` warmup diff, `5.68e-14` max residual identity diff). |
| **`SwiftStats` Sample Moments (`ddof=1`)** | Sample denominator convention vs SciPy population default required explicit contract alignment. | Documented and verified sample skewness & excess kurtosis (`ddof=1`) against translated SciPy across small (`n=5`) and `100k` series. | Skewness diff `2.71e-17`, excess kurtosis diff `2.13e-14` (`100,000` values). |
| **TreeSHAP Scope & Harness Cleanup (`G-035`, `G-036`)** | Legacy `--research` suite reported unvalidated in-process checks; TreeSHAP scope boundaries needed formal gating. | Retired legacy `--research` runners; formally documented GBDT regressor (`G-035`) and class-probability (`G-036`) deferrals. | `39/39` standardized scientific workloads + `6/6` internal certificates passed. |

### Deferred Coverage & Comparator Boundaries

- **G-035 (Deferred)**: `TreeSHAP` for `GradientBoostedTreesRegressor` remains unsupported.
- **G-036 (Deferred)**: Class-targeted probability `TreeSHAP` remains deferred (the classifier adapter explains encoded class-label output).
- **Pandas Wampler Failures (`4` Comparator Cases)**: CPU acceptance passed `14/16` profiles cleanly and recorded `4` **pandas** mismatches on `nist-wampler4` and `nist-wampler5` across `numerical-binary64` and `numerical-conformance` (`1.0000000028854616 != 1.0`, `1.0000003429656807 != 1.0`); SwiftSci passed `202/202` engine-case executions.
- **Skipped Conditional Llama Parity Checks**: `3` conditional live-checkpoint Swift Llama checks (chat IDs without duplicate BOS, pinned byte-exact decoding, live HuggingFace MLX `tokenizers`/`mlx_lm` parity) were skipped in this offline release run.
