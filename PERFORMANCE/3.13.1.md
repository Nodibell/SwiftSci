# SwiftSci 3.13.1 Performance Overview

> [!WARNING]
> **v3.13.2 Audit & Harness Retirement Notice**: Legacy `--research` benchmarks (`SwiftSciBenchmarks --research`, `Benchmarks/Python/benchmarks.py`, `Benchmarks/CSVAcceleration/`) included in the historical `3.13.1` archive were **retired and removed in `v3.13.2`**. In particular, legacy ARIMA (`Hannan-Rissanen` OLS vs Statsmodels MLE) and Holt-Winters (SSE vs likelihood) wall-clock ratios are non-comparable, and whole-process `Peak RSS` includes Python runtime overhead. For published AC-power benchmarks, see [`PERFORMANCE/3.13.2.md`](3.13.2.md).

Evaluated on Apple M4 Pro (`arm64`, 24 GiB RAM, AC power, `macOS 27.2.1` build `26B131`), Swift `6.4.2` Release (`-O -whole-module-optimization`, `MTL_FAST_MATH=NO`, `MTL_MATH_MODE=SAFE`), vs Python `3.14.6` (`Pandas 3.0.6`, `NumPy 2.5.3`, `SciPy 1.18.1`, `PyArrow 25.0.1`) for standardized `bench.py` suites and Python `3.11.9` (`Scikit-Learn 1.8.0`, `Statsmodels 0.14.6`, `SHAP 0.51.0`, `PyTorch 2.11.0`, `NLTK 3.10.3`) for patched-path and retired `--research` runs.

## Highlights & What Changed in 3.13.1

- **Dedicated `.inner` Compact SIMD Hash Join (`-79.5%` Standard / `-84.0%` Mixed vs `3.12.0`)**: Added a dedicated `.inner` path in [`DataFrame+SIMDJoin.swift`](../Sources/SwiftDataFrame/Core/DataFrame+SIMDJoin.swift) skipping `BitSet`/unmatched tracking and consolidating gathers, lowering `100k` inner join latency from `10.64 ms` / `13.81 ms` (`3.12.0`) to **`2.19 ms`** (`standard`) and **`2.21 ms`** (`mixed`).
- **Mixed-Storage Scalar Gather Delegation (`-27.9%` Mixed Filter vs `3.12.0`)**: Routing [`TypedColumn.swift`](../Sources/SwiftDataFrame/Columns/TypedColumn.swift) `gatheredScalar` through contiguous `gatheredNumeric` lowered `mixed-medium-filter` from `0.414 ms` (`3.12.0`) to **`0.298 ms`** (**SwiftSci 2.97×** vs Pandas `0.886 ms`).
- **Pre-Reserved Single-String `CSVWriter` & Quoted Null-Token CSV Read**: Direct `ColumnAccessor` extraction into a pre-reserved buffer ([`CSVWriter.swift`](../Sources/SwiftDataFrame/IO/CSVWriter.swift)) plus quoted null-token preservation delivered **3.45–4.94× faster writes** (`9.88–17.53 ms` vs Pandas `48.80–60.42 ms`) and **3.02–3.92× faster null-token reads** (`3.33–3.51 ms`) on clean idle hardware.
- **Correctness-Hardened Kernels (`SwiftStats`, `SwiftPreprocessing`)**: Upper-tail gamma `Chi-Square GOF` (**65.67×**), Royston AS R94 `Shapiro-Wilk` (**1.05–20.02×**), calibrated `Lilliefors` KS (**1.11–11.52×**), and `RFE` (**62.40×**) beat Python references, while stricter two-pass/OOF paths (`TargetEncoder_OOF`, shifted `VarianceThreshold`, `PolynomialFeatures`, `TypedColumn_Variance`, canonical `unique`) trade raw speed for mathematical correctness.
- **OS/Toolchain Variance Audit (`3.12.0` → `3.13.1`)**: Flagged `+5.0%` to `+16.9%` `standard`/`migration` drifts on `macOS 27.2.1` / `Swift 6.4.2` affected unmodified Python/Pandas too (`5/14` `standard` and `19/36` `migration` regressions) with zero kernel diff; clean follow-up `comparison-suite-20261114-2033` restored `medium-csv-read` to `2.37 ms` and `medium-sort` to `2.27 ms`.

## Methodology & Reproducibility

- **Separated Tiers**: Standardized `bench.py` process-isolated campaigns (`3` batches × `5` samples, `2` warmups), targeted idle microbenchmarks (`3 × 7 + 2` warmups), and retired single-process `--research` runs are strictly separated and never mixed.
- **Reproduce Current Suites**: Legacy `--research` runners were removed in `v3.13.2`. Use `Benchmarks/.venv-standardized/bin/python Benchmarks/Tools/bench.py` and `Benchmarks/Tools/scientific_validation_suite.py` (see [`Benchmarks/README.md`](../Benchmarks/README.md)), inspect raw data in [`performance.deep.md`](../Benchmarks/Reports/v3.13.1/performance.deep.md), and verify numerical parity in [`ACCURACY/3.13.1.md`](../ACCURACY/3.13.1.md).
