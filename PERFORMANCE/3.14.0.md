## Highlights & What Changed in 3.14.0

- **`OneVsRestClassifier.predictProbability` (`2.94×` Faster Inference)**: Vectorized probability scoring across `500 × 16` (`4` classes) completes in **`0.069 ms`** median vs scikit-learn **`0.203 ms`** (**SwiftSci 2.94×** faster), while full 1,000-epoch Swift gradient-descent `fit` (`1000 × 16`, `4` classes) takes `192.265 ms` vs scikit-learn `LogisticRegression` (`3.820 ms`, **Python 50.33×**).
- **`TreeSHAP` GBDT & Multiclass Probability Explanations (`G-035`, `G-036`)**: On `50` held-out instances, `GradientBoostedTreesRegressor` batch `TreeSHAP` (`50` trees, `depth=4`) runs in `3.709 ms` vs `shap.TreeExplainer` `1.045 ms` (**Python 3.55×**), and `RandomForestClassifier` multiclass per-class `TreeSHAP` (`30` trees, `depth=5`, `3` classes) runs in `21.727 ms` vs `shap.TreeExplainer` `1.316 ms` (**Python 16.51×**) in pure Swift without Python/C++ dependencies.
- **Full 4-Engine AC `production-default` Suite (`10,300` Samples)**: All `6` standardized comparison campaigns (`standard`, `extended`, `public-data`, `nist`, `migration`, `tabular-migration`) ran on AC power with native engine thread defaults (`5` rotating process rounds per case, `0` failures).
- **Correctness-Cleared Scientific Benchmarking (`39/39`)**: Cross-engine scientific speedups are reported only for stages cleared by scientific accuracy and comparator-equivalence checks.

## Methodology & Reproducibility

- **Timing Boundaries**: Worker wall time covers materialized output (CSV parsing is inside timed reads; DuckDB fetches into Arrow/NumPy inside timing). Fixture setup, startup, imports, 2 warmups, and validation are excluded. Standardized comparison batches run 5 fresh rotating processes per engine (`25` samples per engine-case).
- **Interpretation Caveats**: Ratios are suppressed where timing falls below 1 µs (NIST mean/variance/stddev, small RAG summary, Fixed-parameter Holt-Winters (`N=500`) infer at `0.792 µs`, Small-sample descriptive moments (`N=5`)) or where optimization objectives differ (Optimized Holt-Winters (`N=500`): SwiftSci `0.276 ms` vs statsmodels `48.660 ms`). Whole-process Peak RSS in raw reports includes runtime/library startup overhead.
