### Measurement Methodology Disclosures

1. **Standardized CLI Sweeps:** Independent OS process batches audited via `bench.py audit` (`certificate: passed` on all 6 sweeps).
2. **Scientific Validation Suite:** `5` independent worker processes × `5` measured samples per process + `2` warmup iterations (`median of process medians`, IQR dispersion reported).
3. **Sub-microsecond Suppression (`RES < 1 us`):** Stages with SwiftSci median elapsed time `< 1000 ns` (`< 0.0010 ms`) are marked resolution-limited and excluded from speedup claims (e.g. NIST univariate 1-D benchmarks, ARIMA `inf` on small series in micro-stage, `stats_ddof1_small5`).
4. **Memory Metric Scope:** Peak RSS values are **whole-process** `ru_maxrss` (`swift_process_peak_rss` / `python_process_peak_rss`), capturing process runtime/framework baseline overhead plus workload heap allocations rather than isolated allocation deltas.
5. **Cross-OS Comparison Boundary:** Legacy `v3.12.0` baselines ran on `macOS 26B123 / Swift 6.4`, whereas `v3.13.2-candidate` ran on `macOS 26B131 / Swift 6.4.2`. `bench.py compare` rightly rejects mixed-environment diffs; any reference to `v3.12.0` timings below carries an explicit OS/toolchain boundary caveat.
