# SwiftSci 3.12.0 Performance Overview

> [!WARNING]
> **v3.13.2 Historical Audit Notice**: Audited `standard-3.12.0` and `migration-3.12.0` tabular runs were executed on Apple M4 Pro (`arm64`, `macOS 27.2`, Swift `6.4` Release) against Python `3.14.6` (`Pandas 3.0.6`, `NumPy 2.5.3`, `SciPy 1.18.1`, `PyArrow 25.0.1`). Legacy `--research` entries (`Holt-Winters >5000×`, `ARIMA(1,1,1) >9000×`, `SwiftLLM 0.121 ms vs 1.450 ms`) and 1M-row experimental tables (`Kiraa`) come from retired unvalidated harnesses removed in `v3.13.2`; ARIMA uses Hannan-Rissanen OLS (not Statsmodels MLE), Holt-Winters optimizes SSE (not likelihood), and whole-process `Peak RSS` includes Python runtime overhead (~170–230 MiB).

---

## Highlights & What Changed in 3.12.0

- **21.2× Faster Streaming CSV Ingestion (`2.21 ms` vs `46.80 ms` in `3.11.0`)**: Scoped record batch discovery, on-demand streaming chunk materialization, and SIMD structural scans for unquoted CSV records turned chunked streaming from `Pandas 2.11×` (`22.18 ms`) in `3.11.0` into **SwiftSci 5.81×** (`2.21 ms` vs `12.82 ms`), alongside streaming filter (`2.58 ms`, **5.49×**) and streaming groupby (`2.94 ms`, **8.27×**, `18.6 MiB` vs `220.4 MiB` process Peak RSS) (PR #85–#87).
- **1.70× Faster Full CSV Read (`2.20 ms` vs `3.76 ms` in `3.11.0`)**: Bounded ingestion metadata, SIMD structural flag narrowing, and word-level validity flushing accelerated 100k-row CSV parsing to **`2.20 ms`** (**SwiftSci 4.50×** vs Pandas `9.92 ms`), cutting CSV Peak RSS from `48.7 MiB` to **`32.2 MiB`** (PR #85–#87).
- **1.29× Faster `Double` Sorting (`2.14 ms` vs `2.75 ms` in `3.11.0`)**: End-to-end compact `Double` buffer retention without optional boxing across decoding, gathering, filtering, and adaptive radix sorting moved 100k `Double` sort ahead of NumPy (**SwiftSci 1.35×**, `2.14 ms` vs `2.90 ms`) and cut tabular Peak RSS by **25–34%** (PR #86, #88).
- **Prepared Classification, PCA & Tile Preprocessing (`SwiftWorkflow`, `SwiftML`, `SwiftCluster`)**: Added zero-expansion `PreparedNumericBatch` paths across `PCA`, `LogisticRegression`, `LinearSVC`, `ClassificationPipeline`, and the 15th module `SwiftWorkflow` (`PCAClassificationWorkflow`), plus unrolled tile preprocessing (PR #82–#84, #89, #93, #98–#101).
- **Faster Spearman & ROC-AUC**: Spearman correlation improved **3.61×** vs `3.11.0` (`2.90 ms` vs `10.48 ms`), and ROC-AUC improved **1.22×** (`2.12 ms` vs `2.58 ms`).

## Methodology & Reproducibility

- **Methodology v2**: Operates on SHA-256 verified binary/CSV fixtures across shared workload, library-to-library, and hardware-specialized tiers; `1,729` tests and `100%` DocC (`2,525/2,525`) recorded at release.
- **Reproduce Current Suites**: Legacy `--research` scripts (`SwiftSciBenchmarks --research`, `Benchmarks/Python/benchmarks.py`, `Benchmarks/generate_fixtures.py`) were retired in `v3.13.2`. Use `Benchmarks/Tools/bench.py` and `Benchmarks/Tools/scientific_validation_suite.py` (see [`Benchmarks/README.md`](../Benchmarks/README.md)), and inspect numerical validation in [`ACCURACY/3.12.0.md`](../ACCURACY/3.12.0.md).
