## Highlights & What Changed in 3.13.3

- **768-D `MLPClassifier` Mini-Batch BLAS Acceleration (`2.57×` Faster Fit)**: Replacing scalar inner training loops with `Accelerate` (`cblas_dgemm` / `vDSP`) mini-batch forward/backward passes cuts `MLPClassifier` fit time (`N=512, D=768, C=5, hidden=[64], batchSize=32, maxIter=20`) to **`40.312 ms`** median vs scikit-learn **`103.639 ms`** (**SwiftSci 2.57×** faster; `train_accuracy = 1.0000`), while `predictProbability` runs in `0.504 ms` vs scikit-learn `0.444 ms` (**Python 1.14×**).
- **Wide-Layer Batched MLP Inference (`256 KiB` Scratch Budget)**: Expanded batched `cblas_dgemm` prediction width coverage from `<= 512` up to `<= 8,192` while capping per-call scratch memory at `256 KiB`.
- **Full 4-Engine AC `production-default` Suite (`10,300` Samples)**: All `6` standardized comparison campaigns (`standard`, `extended`, `public-data`, `nist`, `migration`, `tabular-migration`) ran on AC power with native engine thread defaults (`5` rotating process rounds per case, `0` failures).
- **Correctness-Cleared Scientific Benchmarking (`39/39`)**: Cross-engine scientific speedups are reported only for stages cleared by scientific accuracy and comparator-equivalence checks.

## Methodology & Reproducibility

- **Timing Boundaries**: Worker wall time covers materialized output (CSV parsing is inside timed reads; DuckDB fetches into Arrow/NumPy inside timing). Fixture setup, startup, imports, 2 warmups, and validation are excluded. Standardized comparison batches run 5 fresh rotating processes per engine (`25` samples per engine-case).
- **Interpretation Caveats**: Ratios are suppressed where timing falls below 1 µs (`nist-mean/variance/stddev`, `tiny-rag-summary`, Fixed-parameter Holt-Winters (`N=500`) infer at `0.750 µs`, Small-sample descriptive moments (`N=5`)) or where optimization objectives differ (Optimized Holt-Winters (`N=500`): SwiftSci `0.271 ms` vs statsmodels `48.074 ms`). Whole-process Peak RSS in raw reports includes runtime/library startup overhead.
